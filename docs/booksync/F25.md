# BookSync F25 cover references

Implements PLAN F25 on F12/F21/F23, following approved Home revision 16's
placeholder/post-paint behavior and Android's companion-owned cover references.
This is a service slice: no new screen, reader hook, live transport activation,
server write, or sync owner. F36 consumes the neutral Library intents/values.
The existing offline Library retains its safe unavailable defaults until then.

`Library::requestCover(BookKey)` is a post-paint intent. The future account/catalog
facade resolves that key against its current published generation and captures a
worker-only `CoverSpec`: opaque server/account/book tokens and a nonzero 32-byte
artwork revision. Artwork revision is independent of the EPUB's verified source
SHA-256: it must change when the catalog's artwork version changes. Unknown or
removed artwork has zero revision and retains the typographic placeholder.
No adapter URL, credential, filesystem path or mutable job reaches UI.

One cooperative `CoverJobs` instance belongs to the account/catalog owner and is
serialized with F21/F23/account transitions. `request`, `reference`, and `copy`
perform no I/O. Only `tick`, called after paint, admits F11 transfer/cover/TLS/worker
resources and opens the source. It stages at most 512 KiB through one reused
1 KiB member buffer. Both declared and streaming lengths are checked; negative,
oversized, truncated, failed-framing and unauthorized responses cannot publish.
The private `server/bookorbit/Cover` resolver revalidates the exact scoped artwork
revision and accepts only same-origin relative paths, no redirects, and JPEG
responses. Its transport must have bounded I/O timeouts and finish authorization.
No concrete HTTP/session implementation is activated by this slice, matching F23.

Transport closes and returns its TLS token before decode. The staging writer
closes before one reader opens, preserving the hardware's single-reader contract.
A segment-length-checked baseline JPEG preflight rejects unsupported SOF modes,
precision/components and dimensions before decoder admission. Input is capped at
512 KiB, each dimension at 2048, source pixels at 2,097,152, output at 128x192
packed monochrome (3072 bytes). JPEGDEC's fixed workspace is reused, with coarse
1/2, 1/4 or 1/8 decode and bounded destination sampling. Callback checks honor
reading cancellation. Progressive, lossless, PNG, GIF, WebP and malformed data
remain placeholders; there is no alternate heavyweight decoder fallback.

Four prepared thumbnails form a fixed FIFO working set, owned by the service
lifetime. Exact repeated requests coalesce or reuse a prepared reference without
transport/decode. References are non-reused process IDs, never cache paths.
The facade calls `observe` on committed artwork changes/removal before presenting
the new catalog; this invalidates old references and any obsolete queued work.
Requesting a new revision also invalidates the old one. Account transitions call
`adopt` with F12's new owner/epoch, clear references and drain the old acquisition.
A second scope cannot reuse the same epoch, and A→B→A cannot revive old IDs.
Eviction, restart and service teardown invalidate disposable references. This
slice does not add a durable cover store or change EPUB cache formats; cold
missing covers remain placeholders and are reacquired after paint.

A view owns an F12 Owner-policy Delivery, polls the immutable result outside
paint, and retires on Back/close/replacement. Retiring suppresses presentation
without canceling preparation for other consumers. Workers never retain a view
callback, request rendering, navigate, or draw. Only an eligible, live delivery
may request a changed tile redraw. Reading entry must drain `busy()` before
destroying the service or releasing its private staging path. Failed network
teardown keeps display ownership and is retried on later ticks.

Allocate the fixed `CoverJobs` workspace fallibly once for its service lifetime
and release it before reader entry. The <=40 KiB bound includes JPEGDEC, four
3 KiB bitmaps, selection metadata and the 1 KiB transfer buffer: these do not
belong on a 2–4 KiB task stack or in permanent static DRAM on C3. The service
itself makes no payload-sized or per-chunk heap allocations. HAL/client runtime
allocations remain their owners' responsibility. The three physical builds
retain the dormant constructor/destructor, worker, decoder and adapter for real
image/map measurements. Simulator logs report actual workspace size and bounds;
native heap/largest-block/stack measurements cannot represent ESP32 acceptance.

Validation: `CoverJobsTest` compiles the production worker, adapter and pinned,
patched real JPEGDEC. `test/simulator/cover_jobs.py` runs acquisition/cache reuse,
revision invalidation, account transitions, retired views, declared/streaming
oversize and malformed-image scenarios in each of the three profiles. CI runs
these with the retained host, reader and simulator suites and the 128 KiB reserve
gate. The public-boundary self-tests cover bounded bitmap copies and reject
pointers, stored references and opaque output handles.

Maintainer hardware verification after F36 connects the approved catalog UI:
on X4 Pro, X4 Classic and C3 X3/X4, enter Home offline and confirm immediate
placeholder/text paint. Connect and acquire supported thumbnails afterward;
change an artwork revision, remove artwork, switch account A→B→A, and leave the
view before completion. Only a still-live visible tile may repaint. Reading must
stay in focus and refuse acquisition; no old artwork may reappear. Repeat with
oversized/progressive/malformed images and a full SD card: keep the placeholder,
log the failure, and release storage/display ownership. Measure internal free
heap/largest block and task high-water during transfer and decode separately;
S3 PSRAM does not replace internal-heap evidence. No EPUB cache reset is needed.
Agents perform no device/serial checks.
